# Market research — career structure tools

How similar products model and present a career structure, and what EnPath can borrow.
Evidence is from public marketing and help pages only; admin UIs are behind logins, so layout
details are inferred from product copy, not seen. Verify with a trial or demo before relying on them.

## Summary

| Product | Model | What stands out | Relevance to EnPath |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Progression** | Tracks → Positions, Skills with levels | "Eagle-view" of the framework: all skills, positions and required levels juxtaposed in one view. "Compare positions" side by side. Starter skill library + AI assistant for writing skills | Closest match to EnPath Setup. The one-view framework is the Overview idea |
| **Lattice (Grow)** | Tracks → Levels; Competency Library; expectations per level | Admin > Grow > Tracks / Competencies. **Track templates** (IC and Manager, pre-loaded competencies, levels L2–L5 or L1–L6). Job levels can **auto-sync** from HR data into tracks | Templates solve the empty-start problem. Admin lives in a separate Admin area, like Operations → Setup |
| **Leapsome** | Teams × Levels → Skills with level definitions | **AI drafts a whole competency framework from a chat**: "Describe your roles, values, and ambitions in a chat". Admin must approve before it goes live | Direct precedent for the right-hand chat panel + Preview → Confirm |
| **Culture Amp (Career Paths)** | Job groups → Roles, Competencies with levels per role/competency pair | Employees "compare different roles… see how expectations change". Competencies link to learning (Skills Coach, Go1) | Role-by-competency level setting = EnPath Expectations grid. Learning links ≈ My Actions |
| **Pave / Ravio** (comp tools) | Job families × Levels (P1–P6 etc.) = **role grid** | Whole company as one grid: families as rows, a shared level axis as columns | Pattern for a company-wide Overview. Not a career-development tool itself |

Other names seen, not researched: Zavvy (now part of Deel), Workleap, TalentGuard, iMocha.
Open-source ladders for content reference: progression.fyi.

## Patterns worth borrowing

1. **One place to see the whole framework** (Progression eagle-view, Pave role grid). Every
   Position against a shared level axis. Fits EnPath's Overview.
2. **Edit one Position/track at a time**, levels × competencies in one grid (Lattice, Culture Amp).
   Matches the workspace sketch.
3. **Templates and libraries to start from** (Lattice track templates, Progression starter skills).
   EnPath's PRDs have nothing for this. An empty Setup is the hardest first run.
4. **AI drafts by chat, human approves** (Leapsome). Validates the chat panel direction and
   PRD-004's human-confirmation rule.
5. **Compare positions side by side** (Progression). Useful for designing cross-position Career
   Paths (Engineer → Manager).

## Where EnPath differs

- Competitors tie frameworks to **performance reviews**; EnPath explicitly does not (PRD-018 BR-01).
- EnPath has **explicit Career Paths** as their own object, including cross-position moves.
  Most competitors imply the path from level order within a track.
- EnPath's **Preview → Confirm + audit** on every write is stricter than anything seen publicly.
